brackets, card layouts and the camera-rect convention are defined there). 120 BPM, 15 bars, bar N
starts at (N-1) x 2.0 s. The cutdown skips the red test (it uses only the passing re-run) and
shows three feature cards. Its score is its own arrangement (`score.md` §5.2), not a cut of the
60 s mix.

## 3. Notes

- Deviation from the first draft: no wide shot of the re-run in bar 5. The re-run in the
  recording still has the red step on screen above it, so a wide jump cut would show `HIT
  exit 101` without the story. Instead the `a` answer holds through the air gap and the drop is a
  hard cut straight to `exit 0` (the transcript has already settled; clip 690–696, a transient
  `exit 0` on the running step, is never shown).
- Card 11 headline differs from the trailer (it merges cards 4–6) because it is the only modes
  card. All three items are verified (`direction.md` §6).
- Audio cue times above are absolute and match `v2/cutdown30/events.json`.
